---
title: Journaling, still in progress
date: 2026-08-09
summary: It usually works. I'm still learning how to do it effectively.
---

I've been trying to journal properly for a while now. Not a diary,
nothing sentimental, more a running record: what I did, what broke,
what I'd do differently next time. And it usually works. The problem is
that "usually" is doing a lot of work in that sentence, and I'm still
learning how to do this effectively.

The writing part is easy. It's everything around it that's hard.
Remembering to do it while the day is still fresh. Being honest instead
of impressive. And actually going back to read what I wrote, because a
journal you never read is just a more expensive way of forgetting.

My rules so far are simple. Write something even if it's one line. No
minimum word count, or it becomes a chore and dies. Be specific enough
that future me knows what I meant. Read it back sometimes, even if it's
uncomfortable seeing how you were feeling.

It usually works. Some weeks I miss three days in a row and tell myself
it's fine, because it is, mostly. The part I've got right is that doing
it badly beats not doing it at all, and the rest is still in progress.
